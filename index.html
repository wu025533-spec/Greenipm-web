About Green IPM

Based in Hong Kong Science Park
A green pest control R&D company

Based in Hong Kong Science Park, GreenIPM HK focuses on the research, development, and commercialization of green integrated pest management technologies.

With Hong Kong as the hub for our R&D and quality control, we leverage international proven product resources and strategic manufacturing partners to provide AI-based, green, and sustainable pest management solutions for agriculture, warehousing, forestry, and public health.

Sustainability as a Core Mission

"We believe a better present requires more transparent management, not inflated promises, but the responsibility we keep building step by step."

GreenIPM HK is research-led and focused on the development and commercialization of green integrated pest management technologies. Our goal is to help clients embed sustainability into operations, improving pest control efficiency through technology while taking long-term responsibility for environmental, social, and governance impact.

Ecology First, Green Control

We treat the reduction of unnecessary chemical dependence as a long-term priority, using lower-risk, lower-residue, and more sustainable methods to address pest problems.

Protecting Health, Serving Society

From food safety to storage safety, we aim to improve the quality of health risk management through reliable, transparent, and explainable pest control methods.

Better Governance, Responsible Operations

Through clearer records, stronger processes, and verifiable mechanisms, we help clients elevate pest control from one-off treatment to ongoing governance.
